## What is the Humphrey Field Analyzer (HFA)?

The HFA is an advanced diagnostic device that measures your **peripheral (side) vision** — also known as your visual field. While a standard eye chart only tests your central vision, the HFA maps the full range of what you can see, detecting blind spots or areas of reduced sensitivity that you may not be aware of.

This is especially important because many eye diseases, particularly glaucoma, affect **side vision first** — often without any noticeable symptoms.

---

## Why is Visual Field Testing Important?

Most people believe that as long as they can read the eye chart, their vision is fine. But central vision (the part tested by a standard eye chart) is only a small fraction of your total visual field. Glaucoma and certain retinal conditions can cause significant damage to peripheral vision while central vision remains intact.

Without visual field testing, these conditions can progress silently — sometimes for years — before they are detected. By the time symptoms become noticeable, damage may already be significant and irreversible.

The HFA allows us to:
- **Detect** diseases in their earliest stages
- **Monitor** how a condition is progressing over time
- **Evaluate** how well treatment is working
- **Tailor** treatment plans to each patient's specific needs

## What Happens During an HFA Test?

Many patients feel anxious about diagnostic tests, but the HFA is simple, painless, and non-invasive.

**Here is what to expect:**

1. You sit comfortably in front of the HFA machine and place your chin on a rest
2. One eye is covered while the other looks at a fixed central target
3. Small, dim lights flash at various locations across your field of vision
4. You press a button each time you see a light
5. The machine creates a detailed map showing where your vision is normal and where it may be reduced

**Duration:** 5–10 minutes per eye
**Discomfort:** None — the test is completely non-invasive
**Preparation:** No special preparation needed; you can drive yourself home

The machine's advanced software compares your results to age-matched normal values, giving your doctor a precise, objective measurement of your visual health.
