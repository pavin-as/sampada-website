## Getting to Know Diabetic Retinopathy

Diabetic retinopathy (DR) is a complication caused by diabetes, which damages the small blood vessels in the retina. With India having the second-highest diabetes rate worldwide, cases of retinopathy are increasing.

The challenge with diabetic retinopathy is that its symptoms often go unnoticed. Many people only seek help after they observe changes in their vision, by which point the damage may have advanced to a serious or even irreversible stage.

Despite the risks, regular retinal checkups are often neglected. Many patients don't realize that the retina is one of the organs most vulnerable to the effects of prolonged high blood sugar.

---

## Spotting Issues Early with Retinal Imaging

Retinal imaging, also known as retinography or fundus imaging, captures detailed images of the retina in just seconds. The process is quick, painless, and non-invasive.

These images enable specialists to detect early retinal changes associated with diabetes, even if your vision appears unaffected.
